# Project Context

This project is a short practical note/blog-post-style document about things to look out for when working with data for economics research projects, and sometimes data work more generally. It is mainly written from R work, often with spatial data, and partly for people who know the author's past project context, including a professor the author worked with.

The goal is to preserve rough material and gradually shape it into a readable practical note. It is allowed to read like a personal notebook rather than a formal RA manual. Some parts may later become one or more audience-specific Codex skills, but this draft should keep useful project memories, examples, and caveats.

Main working file:

- `ra_lessons.Rmd`

Rendered output is generated locally and does not need to be tracked while the draft is changing often.

General editing preference:

- Preserve the author's wording unless explicitly asked to edit.
- Reorganising sections is okay when it helps the structure, but do not over-optimize the order too early.
- Add examples, checks, or suggestions where useful, but avoid over-specifying the final order too early.
- Keep the document practical and concise rather than turning it into a full methods guide or generic RA handbook.
